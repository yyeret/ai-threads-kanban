# Lean Product Canvas Intent: Content Publishing

## Outcome-Oriented Goal

Turn Yuval's source material, transcripts, drafts, and content-system fixes into published, discoverable, reusable thought leadership that helps the right readers understand his AI transformation and product-operating-model point of view.

Leading indicators:

- Throughput: at least 3 source-to-published or source-to-reused content assets per month.
- Goal fit: at least 80 percent of associated threads directly support ingestion, drafting, improvement, publishing, syndication, or distribution.
- Next-action clarity: every active content thread has a concrete publish/reuse next action, not a generic clarify placeholder.
- Reader clarity: new content threads name the intended reader and surface.

## Customer Segments

- Yuval, turning advisory insight and transcripts into reusable content.
- Potential customers and decision influencers looking for practical AI transformation and flow ideas.
- Syndication partners or external surfaces such as Scrum.org, Every, RSS subscribers, and website readers.

## Problem

There is a lot of source material, but not all of it becomes visible, useful content. Threads mix ingestion, writing, website fixes, RSS plumbing, syndication, and analysis. Without a canvas, "content" becomes too broad and progress is measured as activity rather than publishing or reuse.

## Existing Alternatives

- Leave source material in transcripts or session notes.
- Publish one-off posts without improving reuse or discoverability.
- Fix distribution issues only when they become visible blockers.
- Treat all writing-adjacent work as content even when it belongs to analytics, infra, or client delivery.

## Unique Value Proposition

Source material turns into durable market-facing assets with a clear reader, surface, and reuse path.

## Solution Shape

- Ingest transcripts and source assets into the right repository or vault.
- Draft or refresh posts/pages with reader and outcome clarity.
- Fix publishing mechanics such as RSS, images, metadata, syndication, and AEO/SEO structure.
- Track each content thread by publish/reuse outcome.

## Channels / Surfaces

- `yeret-agility-site`
- RSS feeds and external syndication surfaces
- blog posts, pages, podcast notes, LinkedIn-ready notes
- transcript-ingestion workflows
- shared memory when source material needs future reuse

## Revenue / Value Logic

Better content creates authority, improves discoverability, warms buyers, and turns delivery insight into reusable IP.

## Cost Structure

- Editorial review and source preservation.
- Site, RSS, image, and syndication maintenance.
- Thread cleanup when ingestion, client delivery, and content work blur.

## Unfair Advantage

Yuval has a deep corpus of real advisory work, transformation material, and agent-assisted development history that can produce content with lived specificity.

## Thread Association Guidance

Strong fit:

- The thread moves source material toward a publishable or reusable content artifact.
- The work improves a content surface, syndication path, metadata, feed, article, post, page, or content distribution workflow.
- The thread names a reader, surface, and next publish/reuse action.

Weak fit:

- Transcript ingestion with no downstream content path.
- Website or analytics work where the content outcome is incidental.
- General client-plan analysis that belongs to a customer engagement goal.

Possible misfit:

- Agent-infra, CRM, proposal, prospecting, analytics, or personal-admin work labeled as content because text is involved.

